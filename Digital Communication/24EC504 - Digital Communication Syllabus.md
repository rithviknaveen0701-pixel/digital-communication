## Course Objective

The students will be able to analyze mathematical background required for communication system and understands the concepts of building blocks of digital communication systems.

## Course Outcomes

1. Comprehend the basics of digital communication, sampling and waveform coding techniques such as PCM, DPCM, and DM.
2. Analyze the various sampling techniques, baseband transmission, pulse shaping, PAM signals, ISI, and Nyquist criterion for distortion less transmission.
3. Analyze different digital modulation techniques and compare binary and quaternary modulation methods.
4. Understand spread spectrum techniques, PN sequences, DSSS, and FHSS used in digital communication systems.

## Module 1 — Introduction and Sampling

**Teaching hours: 10**

- Introduction to Digital communication
- Sources and Signals
- Basic signal processing operations in Digital communication
- Channels for Digital communication
- Sampling Process
  - Sampling theorem
  - Quadrature sampling of band pass signals
  - Reconstruction of a message from its samples
  - Signal distortion in sampling
  - Practical aspects of sampling and signal recovery
- TDM (T1 carrier)

## Module 2 — Waveform Coding and Baseband Transmission

**Teaching hours: 10**

### Waveform Coding Techniques

- PCM
- Quantization noise and SNR
- Robust quantization
- DPCM
- DM

### Base-Band Transmission

- Base-Band Shaping For Data Transmission
- Discrete PAM signals
- Power spectra of PAM signals
- ISI
- Nyquist’s criterion for distortion less base band binary transmission
- Correlative coding

## Module 3 — Digital Modulation Techniques

**Teaching hours: 10**

- Introduction
- Digital modulation formats
- Coherent binary modulation techniques
- Coherent Quadrature modulation techniques
- Non-Coherent binary modulation Techniques
- Comparison of binary and quaternary modulation techniques

## Module 4 — Spread Spectrum Modulation

**Teaching hours: 10**

- PN sequences
- Notation of spread spectrum
- Direct sequence spread coherent binary PSK
- Signal space dimensionality and processing gain
- Frequency hop spread spectrum
